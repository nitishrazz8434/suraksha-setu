# Five-minute demonstration

All example reports below are synthetic; keep the training-data banner visible.

1. **Overview:** explain the initial 2,944-person demand, 2,900 available places and 44 unallocated people. Show that site capacity is resource-limited rather than only floor space.
2. **Risk map:** inspect Mundakkai and Chooralmala, toggle layers, pan/zoom and explain the difference between real basemap geography and illustrative hazard overlays.
3. **Incident report:** submit “The Chooralmala bridge link near Meppadi is blocked. 48 people need transport and drinking water.” Confirm settlement, link Chooralmala bridge link, and submit. The report initially remains Pending.
4. **Verification:** verify the linked closure. Reopen Relocation planning; the southern settlements become disconnected from eligible destinations in the illustrative graph. Explain unallocated demand rather than hiding it.
5. **Scenario lab:** test Water supply stress and compare optimized allocation with nearest eligible site. Export the scenario JSON and apply it if desired.
6. **Sites & capacity:** inspect water and sanitation bottlenecks. Change a resource limit and see the proposed allocation update.
7. **Copilot:** ask “Which settlements need attention first?”, “What limits our accommodation capacity?” and “Prepare a district planning brief.” Inspect source chips. Select Hindi for a brief.
8. **Vision workbench:** use the synthetic optical tile. Change the RGB threshold and inspect computed coverage and known synthetic IoU. Explain the distinction between the runtime heuristic and a trained checkpoint.
9. **Reports:** upload a text bulletin or extractable PDF, retrieve a relevant passage through Copilot, export a brief and backup JSON.
10. **Research studio:** import genuine held-out prediction CSVs. The downloadable format example is intentionally a format example, not a model benchmark.

Resolve the synthetic bridge-closure report and apply Baseline assessment to return to the original scenario. Saved plans remain historical snapshots; review their constraints before advancing their status.
